FAQ

Questions we actually get asked.

If yours isn't here, email us at hello@swiftcargo.local.

How do I track a shipment?

Enter your tracking ID or the recipient's email on the home page and click Track. You'll see the current status, position on a map, and the full timeline of updates.

What does a tracking ID look like?

Every shipment gets an ID in the format SHP-XXXXXX, where the last six characters are letters and numbers. You'll receive it by email the moment we log your parcel.

How often are status updates posted?

Every time our team logs a new status for your parcel — typically 2–4 updates per shipment for Standard, more for Express. The moment an update is saved, it appears on the tracking page.

Can I search by email instead of tracking ID?

Yes. If you don't have the tracking ID handy, enter the recipient's email address and we'll pull up the shipment it belongs to.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Tracking information is only visible to people who know the tracking ID or the recipient's email. We don't sell data or expose it publicly.

What if my parcel is delayed?

Every delay is logged as a tracking event with a note. You'll see exactly where it is and what happened — no vague "in transit" updates.
